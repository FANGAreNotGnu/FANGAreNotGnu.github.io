Haoyang Fang (方昊扬)

Applied Scientist @ Amazon AGI (formerly AWS AI)

I am an Applied Scientist based in Seattle, building autonomous AI systems for LLM post-training and foundation models, from end-to-end ML coding agents to adaptive post-training strategy optimization. My recent research interests span post-training strategy discovery (SFT and RFT), Multi-Agent Systems, RAG, and Agentic Coding.

I proposed and led LLMZero, an LLM-agent system that autonomously discovers adaptive, multi-stage RL post-training strategies; I am currently extending the approach to the broader post-training setup.

I also develop frameworks for general end-to-end machine/deep learning automation, such as AutoGluon Assistant (aka MLZero) , a multi-agent system I proposed and led for autonomous end-to-end ML.

Previously, I was a core team member of AutoGluon Multimodal across releases v0.7 to v1.5 .
